Good to know

Course policies

A few house rules that keep both courses in good shape and the day running smoothly for everyone.

Cart rules Spectators Booking terms Refunds & weather

Cart rules

  • Golf carts must stay on paths near tees and greens, and observe any “cart path only” signage posted for wet conditions.
  • Drivers must be at least 16 years old with a valid driver’s license. The renter is responsible for the cart and any damage.
  • Maximum two riders and two bags per cart.
  • Keep carts well clear of bunkers, water hazards, and freshly seeded areas.
  • Private and personal carts are not permitted; please use course carts.

Spectator policy

  • To protect pace of play and player safety, spectators are limited on the course. Please check in at the pro shop before riding or walking along.
  • Spectators must be 18 years or older, and non-playing riders must follow all cart rules above.
  • During tournaments and events, spectator access may be further restricted — the event organizer and pro shop will advise on the day.

Booking terms

  • Tee times may be booked online or by calling the pro shop. Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your time to check in.
  • We reserve the right to move twosomes back one tee time to group twosomes together.
  • Groups larger than eight players are treated as an outing — please arrange these through the pro shop or the outings & events page.
  • If you’re running late or need to change your group size, call ahead so we can adjust the sheet.

Refunds & weather

  • Green fees are refundable if you cancel before your round begins. Prepaid online bookings can be refunded or credited by the pro shop.
  • Weather: if play is suspended or the course is closed, we’ll issue a refund or a rain check for the unplayed holes.
  • Rain checks are issued at the pro shop and can be used at either course.
  • Punch passes and gift cards are non-refundable but do not expire.
