Riverdale, Utah

Mature, tree-lined and tight — a shot-maker’s course winding along the banks of the Weber River.

Where the Bluff is open, Riverside is a puzzle — corridors of mature trees, water on the turn, and greens that reward a well-shaped iron.

Decades of growth have given Riverside its character: tall cottonwoods frame nearly every hole, the Weber River comes into play more than once, and the fairways ask you to place the ball rather than simply hit it far. It stays green and cool through the summer, and the closing stretch along the water is as pretty a finish as you’ll find in northern Utah.

It is a course that grows on you. Locals play it again and again precisely because no two rounds ask quite the same questions.

A course that rewards a plan

Leave the driver in the bag on a few holes, favor the correct side of the fairway, and Riverside opens up. Get greedy, and the trees and the river will remind you why the members love it.

  • 18 holes of tight, tree-lined golf
  • The Weber River in play on several holes
  • Practice green and warm-up area
  • Active men’s and ladies’ leagues

Riverside Driving Range

Mats to hit off and some grass area — 90 yards wide by 300 yards long — plus two practice putting greens.

Summer hours (May–September): half an hour after daylight until one hour before sunset. The range closes early on Monday evenings — two hours before sunset — to clear it for mowing. Call (801) 399-4636 to check range hours.
