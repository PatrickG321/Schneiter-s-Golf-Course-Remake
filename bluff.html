The Bluff plays the way old-country links do: firm, open, and honest, with the wind as a genuine hazard.

Wide, rolling fairways give you room off the tee, but ponds and a handful of well-placed bunkers ask for a plan on the approach. On a clear morning you can see straight across to Antelope Island and the Great Salt Lake, and the greens run true from spring right through the fall. It is a course that welcomes beginners and still gives a low-handicapper plenty to think about.

Alongside the championship layout, the Bluff short course is a friendly place to warm up, bring the kids, or sharpen the part of the game that actually saves shots.

Links golf in West Point

The Bluff sits right in West Point — easy to get to for a quick nine after work or a full round on the weekend.

Bluff Driving Range

An all-grass hitting area with six target greens and two large golf balls to practice to — 120 yards wide by 350 yards long — plus two large practice putting greens.

Summer hours (May–September): half an hour after daylight until one hour before sunset. The range closes early on Monday and Wednesday evenings — two hours before sunset — to clear it for mowing the next morning. Call (801) 773-0731 to check range hours.
