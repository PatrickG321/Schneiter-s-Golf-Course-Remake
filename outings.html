Outings & events

Room for the whole party

From weddings and corporate days to charity tournaments — groups of 8 to 120 players, on two courses run by the same family for three generations.

Whatever the occasion, we handle the details so your group can enjoy the day.

Our team has been organizing golf events in northern Utah since 1961. We block tee times, arrange carts, set up scoring formats, and open the banquet room for the meal and awards afterward. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll build a package around it.

What we host

  • Weddings & receptions — ceremony on the course with the banquet room for the reception.
  • Corporate events — client days, team scrambles, and staff tournaments with catering.
  • Charity tournaments — shotgun starts, sponsorship signage, prizes, and scoring handled for you.
  • Family & group golf — reunions, birthdays, and casual group outings.

The details

  • Groups from 8 to 120 players
  • Banquet room for meals, awards, and receptions
  • Catering and bar service available
  • Shotgun starts and custom scoring formats
  • Available at both the Bluff and Riverside

Enquire about an event

Catering, your way

We don’t do in-house catering — so you’re free to bring in exactly the food your group wants. Organizers arrange their own vendor and schedule delivery for the end of play.

Local favorites that deliver to the course include Great Harvest, J Dawgs, The Smoked Taco, Cafe Zupas, Raising Canes, Costa Vida, and Mo’ Bettahs. Meals are set up in the banquet room at the Bluff or the Pavilion at Riverside.

Start planning

Have a date in mind? Call the pro shop or send an enquiry and we’ll check availability at both courses.
