For young golfers

Junior golf

PGA lesson camps to learn the game, and a Thursday-morning league to play it — safety, etiquette and fun come first.

PGA Junior Golf Lesson Camps

For boys and girls ages 7–14, all levels. Fun-focused camps cover safety, etiquette, basic rules, and the fundamentals of putting, chipping and the full swing with irons and woods.

Monday to Wednesday, $100, including PGA-certified instruction, clubs if needed, range balls, treats each day, fundamental notes with drills, a graduation party with awards, and a PGA Certificate of Completion.

All sessions 8:00–9:30 a.m.:

  • June 1–3
  • June 15–17
  • June 29–July 1
  • July 13–15
  • July 27–29
  • August 10–12

If those dates don’t suit, private lessons are available for small groups from Kirk Abegglen and Dustin Boyington.

Junior Golf Association & League Play

Thursday mornings, ages 10–17. Children under 10 may take part accompanied by an adult or supervisor. Members must have course experience and understand basic rules, safety, etiquette, care of the course and the courtesies of the game. The emphasis is on participation, safety, fun and meeting new friends. Advanced juniors compete on the front nine with tee times from 7:30 a.m.

Season & dates

  • Season runs June 4 to July 30
  • Junior Club Championship July 16
  • Awards party and lunch July 30

Each week

  • 7:00 — check-in
  • 7:30 — shotgun start on the back nine
  • 7:30 — tee times on the front nine

Cost

  • Dues $45
  • Green fees $10, or a 10-round punch pass for $90

Good to know

  • All participants must complete a sign-up and liability waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian.
  • Prizes are awarded weekly, with special awards at the Junior Club Championship.
  • Players must play at least two events to be eligible for the club championship.
  • Participants must have completed PGA Junior Clinics or private lessons and have course playing experience.
  • Youth on Course discounts are not accepted during Thursday morning association play.

Directors: PGA Professional Kirk Abegglen, Julie Schneiter, Nelson Rendon.

How to sign up

Lesson-camp sessions are booked by phone with payment at the time of booking. League play requires an in-person Pro Shop visit to complete the waiver and pay. Please register in advance.
